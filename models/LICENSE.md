The `.glb` files and `parts.json` in this folder are derived from **BodyParts3D**
(© The Database Center for Life Science, DBCLS, Japan — https://lifesciencedb.jp/bp3d/).
They were simplified (mesh decimation), re-oriented and compressed for web use.

## nerves.glb

`nerves.glb` (and the `nerves` entries in `parts.json`) are derived from **Z-Anatomy**
(https://www.z-anatomy.com/, https://github.com/LluisV/Z-Anatomy), licensed under the
Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 licence (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
Z-Anatomy is itself based on BodyParts3D. The meshes were simplified and re-positioned to
line up with the BodyParts3D skeleton used by the rest of this app.

## female/

The files in `female/` are derived from the **HuBMAP Human Reference Atlas (HRA) 3D Reference Object
Library**, Visible Human Female reference organs (https://github.com/hubmapconsortium/ccf-3d-reference-object-library),
licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
They were simplified, renamed for readability and translated so the body stands on y = 0.
